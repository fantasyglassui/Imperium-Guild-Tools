# Member Profiles

Your profile tells the guild who you are: the spec you play, your role, when you usually play, your birthday and a short note.

## Filling yours in
Open your own player card and click **Edit profile**, or type **/warcrest profile**. Fill in what you like (every part is optional) and click **Save and share**.

- **Main spec** and **Role** (Tank, Healer or DPS).
- **Days you usually play**: click the days to turn them on or off.
- **Times**: like `19:00-23:00`, in your time.
- **Birthday**: the month and day. No year is shared.
- **A short note**: anything you want the guild to know.

**Your item level is added automatically** from the gear you're wearing. When your gear changes, it's updated (at most every few hours).

## Seeing everyone's
- Click any guildmate's name for their **player card**: their profile shows under their name.
- The **Profiles** button on the Roster tab (or **/warcrest profiles**) lists everyone's spec, role, item level and play times. Sort by role or item level for a quick gear check before a raid.

## Birthdays and anniversaries
On a member's birthday, the guild hears about it when they're online. Once someone has been in the guild for a year (as far back as Warcrest has seen them), the guild hears about that too.
