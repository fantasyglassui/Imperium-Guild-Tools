# DKP for Members

You earn DKP by showing up to raids and spend it bidding on loot. Your balance is at the top of the **DKP** tab.

## Earning

When an officer starts the raid, everyone in it gets on-time DKP, then more every hour and for each boss kill. Members on standby earn the same. Officers can also award DKP by hand.

Some guilds take a small percentage off everyone's DKP each week (**decay**) so it doesn't pile up and newer members can catch up.

## Checking

- The **DKP** tab lists everyone's DKP and their raid attendance over the last 30 days. Hover a name for their recent changes and any no-shows.
- Switch the view to **Loot history** to see every item won, by whom, for how much, and on which raid night.
- **All changes** lists every DKP change this season.

## Bidding

When an officer auctions an item, a bid window pops up:

1. Type a bid, or use **+1**, **+5** or **+10**, and click **Bid**. You can't bid more DKP than you have.
2. The winner's DKP is taken automatically. **Pass** closes the window.

| Auction type | What you see | Who wins | What they pay |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Open bids | The highest bid and who made it | Highest bid; a bid in the last 5 seconds adds 5 seconds | Their own bid |
| Hidden bids | Only your own bid (you can change it) | Highest bid; earliest wins a tie | Second-highest bid + 1, or the minimum if only one person bid |

If you try to bid on armor or weapons your class can't use, you're asked to confirm first.

## Free rolls

If nobody bids, the window turns into a **Roll** button for 20 seconds. Click it if you want the item; the highest roll wins it for free. Only your first roll counts.

## Linking your alts

Link your alts to your main so they share one DKP balance and one attendance record. You need both characters to do it, so nobody else can claim yours.

1. Log in on your **alt**. On the **DKP** tab, click **Alts**, type your main's name, and click **Ask to link**.
2. Log in on your **main**. Open **Alts** again (the button shows a red number when a request is waiting) and click **Confirm**.

Your alt can now bid with your main's DKP. To undo it, log in on the alt and click **Unlink this alt**, or ask an officer.

## Seasons

Officers occasionally start a new **season**. You keep your DKP; older DKP changes and loot history are archived so the addon stays fast.
