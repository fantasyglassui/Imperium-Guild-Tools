# Recruiting (Officers)

The **Recruit** tab is for officers. Everything on it is shared with every officer.

## The recruit list
Type a player's name and click **Add recruit**, or target them and click **Add target** to fill in their level and class too. Each recruit has a status:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| New | Just added |
| Contacted | You've talked to them (set automatically when you send a guild invite) |
| Trial | In the guild on trial |
| Joined | A full member |
| Declined | Not this time |

Click the status to move them on. Add a note (what they play, who they know) and click **Save**. **Guild invite**, **Whisper** and **Remove** are under the note.

## Trials
Set a recruit to **Trial** when they join. Warcrest tracks the days, and how many raid nights and guild events they came to. When the trial is over, officers are told how it went, so you can set them to Joined or Declined. Trials last 14 days; pick 7, 21 or 30 with the **Trials** button.

## Whispers about joining
When a player outside the guild whispers you about joining ("guild invite?", "are you recruiting?"), they're listed under **Whispered about joining** with an **Add** button, and you get a note in chat.

## The recruiting message
Write the guild's recruiting message once and **Save message**: every officer gets the same one. Pick a channel (Trade, General...) and click **Post** to send it.
