# Guild Wars, Goals and Crafters

## The war board (rival guilds)

The **Wanted** tab has a **Guilds** view: every enemy guild the guild has met, with how many of theirs we've killed, how many of ours they've killed, and the score.

- Which guild an enemy player is in comes from what guildmates see: their nameplates, targets and mouseovers, and the players they kill or die to. Every kill and death report carries the enemy's guild, so everyone's addon learns it.
- Pick a period with the button on the right: this month, last month, the last 7 days or all time.
- Click a guild to see who on our side has hit them hardest, and who on theirs has hit us hardest.

**Officers** can mark a guild as a **rival** (or add one by name before anyone has met them). Rivals are listed first, in orange, and:

- When **3 or more players of a rival guild** are seen together, the guild is alerted where (at most once every 10 minutes per guild). This uses the Wanted alert setting.
- The **weekly recap** says how each war went: "War with <Bloodfang>: we killed 14, they killed 9 (won)."
- Kills of rival guild players count towards the **Bad Blood** and **Blood Feud** achievements.

## Guild goals

Officers set goals for the whole guild on the **Achievements** tab, **Guild** view: what to count, how many, and whether it's for the week or the month. Goals start over every period.

| What a goal can count | |
| --- | --- |
| Enemy players killed | Every guild kill that isn't grey |
| Rival guild kills | Kills of players in rival guilds |
| Raid bosses killed | Guild boss kills |
| Raid nights | Raid nights started |
| Guild events | Events held (not cancelled) |
| Achievements earned | By anyone in the guild |
| Loot handed out | Items won at auctions, rolls and the loot council |

Everyone sees the progress bars, and the guild hears when a goal is reached ("Guild goal reached: Kill 50 enemy players (this week)."). Up to 4 goals at a time; officers remove one with the **x** next to it. Weeks start at the weekly reset (Tuesday).

## The crafters directory

Who in the guild can make what. Open it with **Crafters** on the Roster tab, or `/warcrest craft` (add a word to search straight away, like `/warcrest craft mooncloth`).

- When you open a profession window (or the Enchanting window), the recipes you know are noted and shared with the guild. Only changes are sent, and a crafting spree is sent as one update.
- Guildmates keep everyone's lists, and ask whoever's online for theirs when they log in, so the directory fills in as people play.
- Search by name and filter by profession. Crafters who are online are listed first, in green.
- **Click a recipe to order it**: pick the crafter (online ones first), tick **I'll bring the materials** if you are, add a note, and click **Place order**. Or click **Whisper** to ask them yourself.
- Recipes are kept by item, so they show in your own language.
- Lists not updated for 6 months are dropped.

### Crafting orders

The crafters window's **Orders** view (or `/warcrest orders`) lists the orders for you to make and the ones you've placed.

- **Crafters** click **Accept** or **Decline**, then **Done** when it's made. You hear when an order comes in, even if it arrived while you were offline.
- **Whoever ordered** can **Cancel** it, and hears when the crafter accepts, declines or finishes it.
- Finished orders stay listed for a week; open ones for a month.
