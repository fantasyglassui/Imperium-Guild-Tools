# Getting Started

Install **Warcrest** from CurseForge, and everything else is shared with the guild automatically.

## Install

1. In the CurseForge app, search for **Warcrest** and click **Install**.
2. Start the game and make sure **Warcrest** is ticked in the **AddOns** list on the character screen.
3. Keep it updated. You'll get a reminder in game when a newer version is out, and a **What's new** window after each update (reopen it with `/warcrest updates`).

## Open it

- Click the **guild emblem** on the edge of your minimap. Left-click opens the addon, right-click opens Settings, Shift-click opens Kills. Drag it to move it around the minimap.
- Type `/warcrest` (or `/wc`).
- Press **Esc**, then **Options > AddOns > Warcrest**.

## The tabs

The menu down the left side of the window switches between tabs, and the window opens on whichever tab you used last.

To make the window bigger, drag its **bottom-right corner**: the lists stretch to show more rows. To make everything bigger or smaller, text included, use **Settings > General > Guild window > Size**. **Reset size and position** puts it back to normal.

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| Roster | The guild roster. Officers manage notes, ranks, invites and the Message of the Day. |
| Raids | Scheduled raids and sign-ups, and your current raid group. |
| DKP | Everyone's DKP and attendance, loot history, and your alts. |
| Wishlist | The loot the guild wants, and your own wishlist. |
| Kills | PvP kills by guild members and the Top Head Hunters board. |
| Wanted | Enemy players who keep killing guild members. |
| Achievements | Your achievements and title, and what the guild has earned. |
| Settings | Alerts (for the guild and just for you), quiet mode, the map, the recap, your title and the window's size. |
| Officers | A log of guild changes, with a search and filter (officers only). |
| Addon | Who in the guild has the addon, and which version. |

## Player cards

Click a guild member's name almost anywhere in the window (the Roster, a killer on the Kills tab, Top Head Hunters, the DKP standings or a raid's sign-ups) to open their **player card**: their PvP record and streaks, nemesis, DKP, attendance and recent loot, all in one place. On the Roster, pick someone and click **View player card**, or double-click their name. You can also type `/warcrest card Name`.

## The weekly recap

After the Tuesday reset, you get a short summary of the guild's last week in chat: enemy players killed and the top three head hunters, guild members killed by enemy players, raid nights and bosses, and loot handed out. If you're offline at the reset, it shows at your next login. Turn it off in **Settings** (Weekly recap), where you can also show it again.

## Choose your alerts

Open **Settings**. The **Just for you** column is yours to change; the **Guild-wide** column is set by officers, and anything turned off there is greyed out for everyone.

- **Alert types:** guild PvP deaths, falls and drowning, PvP kills, Wanted sightings, and level-up celebrations.
- **How alerts appear:** a sound, and a message in the middle of the screen. Every alert also shows in chat.
- **Quiet mode:** alerts wait while you're in a dungeon or raid (on by default), and optionally during combat. They're listed in chat once you're out.
- **World map:** guild PvP deaths are marked on the map for 24 hours by default. The slider sets how long, from 1 hour to 7 days.

**Show test alert** at the bottom shows what each kind looks like.
