# Achievements and Titles

The **Achievements** tab shows what you've earned and what you're working towards. Achievements are worked out from the guild's shared history (kills, deaths, raid nights and loot), so every guildmate sees the same ones, and nothing extra needs setting up.

## Seeing them

- **PvP** and **Raids & more** show tiles for each achievement: gold when earned, with the date, or your progress so far (like 12 / 25).
- **Guild** lists achievements earned across the guild, newest first, and who has the most. Click a name to see all of theirs.
- From anyone's **player card**, click the achievements button to see theirs.
- When a guildmate earns one while you're online, you hear about it. Turn that off in **Settings > Alerts** (Achievements earned).

## Titles

Achievements marked with a title let you show it after your name, like **Lyra the Avenger**. Pick yours at the top of the Achievements tab or in **Settings > General > Your title**. Guildmates see it on your player card and on the Roster. You can only pick titles you've earned, and **No title** turns it off.

## Good to know

- Once your addon has seen an achievement earned, it keeps it, even if an officer clears the kill log or a DKP season archives old history.
- Achievements from before you installed the addon count too, as far back as the guild's shared history goes.
