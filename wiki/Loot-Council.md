# Loot Council

A way to hand out loot by vote instead of DKP. It works whether or not your guild uses DKP, and the winner pays nothing.

## Starting a vote (officers)

1. Be in the raid group.
2. Type `/warcrest council` (or `/warcrest lc`), or click **Loot council** at the top of the Wishlist tab.
3. Shift-click the item into the box, pick how long people have to answer, and click **Start vote**.

## Answering (everyone in the raid)

A small window opens for everyone in the raid with Warcrest. Click **Need** (you'd use it now), **Greed** (you'd take it if nobody needs it) or **Pass**. You can change your answer until time runs out, and you'll see the result in the same window.

## Voting and awarding (officers)

Officers in the raid see everyone who said Need or Greed, with:

- their **wishlist** mark for the item (Need or Want),
- **attendance**: raid nights in the last 30 days,
- **items** won in the last 30 days,
- **DKP**, if the guild uses it,
- and the **votes** so far.

Click a name to vote for them; click again to take the vote back. The officer who started the vote picks the winner (the most-voted is picked for them) and clicks **Award**. The win goes in the loot history, marked "council". **Cancel vote** ends it with nobody getting the item.
