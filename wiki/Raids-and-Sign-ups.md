# Raids and Sign-ups

Open the **Raids** tab, pick a raid on the left, choose your role, and click **Accept**, **Tentative** or **Decline**.

## Signing up

1. Pick a raid on the left, then pick **Tank**, **Healer** or **DPS** on the switch under its details.
2. Click **Accept**, **Tentative** or **Decline**. You can change your answer any time before the raid.
3. If you accepted or are tentative, you get a reminder 15 minutes before the start, and an invite when officers send them.

Times are shown in your own time zone.

## Who's coming

Under the sign-up counts, the Raids tab lists who has accepted by class, and names in the sign-up list are in class colors. An orange warning shows what the raid is short of:

- **Tanks:** 2 wanted once 10 or more have accepted, otherwise 1.
- **Healers:** about one for every five players.
- **Classes:** once 10 have accepted, any class with max-level members in the guild that nobody has signed up with.

Click a name in the sign-up list to open their **player card** (their attendance, DKP and more).

Hover the warning for the full breakdown, including tentative sign-ups by role. Raids with gaps have an orange **!** in the Upcoming raids list. If you can play a role the raid is missing, now's the time to say so.

## Plan

Switch the Raids tab to **Plan** to see the selected raid's groups. Officers put people into groups 1 to 8 or on the bench: click a name, then a group (or the bench). **Auto-fill** places everyone who accepted and isn't placed yet: tanks in group 1, a healer in each group, then everyone else, up to the raid size you pick. Tentative sign-ups and anyone over the size go on the bench. **Clear** starts over. The arrows step between upcoming raids.

Everyone can see the plan, with their own name picked out. At raid time, **Invite sign-ups** invites only the people in groups 1 to 8, and **Arrange raid** moves everyone in the raid into their planned group (you need to be raid leader or assistant, and out of combat).

## Raid night recap

When an officer clicks **End raid**, everyone in the guild with Warcrest gets a summary in chat, and a banner says it's there:

- how long the raid night ran,
- the bosses killed, with guild first kills marked,
- the loot handed out and who won it (and for how much DKP, or by council or roll),
- how many attended, and who was on standby,
- and the DKP you earned that night.

Type `/warcrest raidrecap` to see the last one again. Officers can turn recaps off for the guild in Settings > Alerts (Raid night recaps); anyone can turn them off just for themselves in the You column.

## Calendar

The **Calendar** tab (on the left, under Events, or `/warcrest calendar`) shows a month at a glance: raids scheduled (gold), raid nights held (green, with how many bosses died) and past raids. Hover a day for the details, or click a raid to open it. The arrows move between months; **Today** comes back.

## Progress

Switch to **Progress** to see how far the guild has got in each raid, like **4 / 13 bosses** in Hyjal Summit. Pick a raid for its bosses: the guild's first kill of each, how many times it's died, and the last kill. Hover a boss to see who was there for the first kill.

A boss kill counts for the guild when **5 or more guild members, at least half the raid**, kill a raid boss. One guildmate in the raid records it for everyone. The first kill of each boss is announced to the guild (**Guild first!**), and so is clearing a raid for the first time.

### Wipes and kill times

Every boss pull in a guild raid is recorded, kill or wipe (same rule as kills: 5 or more guild members, at least half the raid). Progress shows the **wipes** and the **best kill time** for each boss; hover a boss for the pulls, the wipes before the first kill and the last kill time. When the guild beats its best time on a boss, the raid hears about it.

## Attunements

**Attunements** (at the bottom of Progress, or `/warcrest attune`) shows who's attuned for **Molten Core, Onyxia, Blackwing Lair and Naxxramas**, and who has the **Upper Blackrock Spire** and **Scholomance** keys. Warcrest checks the quests each member has finished and shares them, so it fills in as guildmates log in with Warcrest. Tick **Level 60 only** to hide alts and levellers.

When you sign up for a raid you aren't attuned for, the sign-up list flags it ("Not attuned (MC)"), so officers can sort it out before raid night. The guild hears when someone gets a new attunement.

## Raid assignments

**Assignments** (at the bottom of Progress, or `/warcrest assign`) holds a plan for each boss: who tanks what, who heals whom, who interrupts. Pick a raid and a boss on the left.

- **Officers** write the plan, a line for each job with the players' names ("Healers: Mira on Thorne, Lyra on groups 1-3"), and click **Save for the guild**. **Post to raid chat** posts it a line at a time.
- **Everyone** sees the plan with their own name picked out, and **Your job** at the top lists just their lines.
- When the boss is pulled (or you target it in the raid), Warcrest reminds you of your job in chat.

## No-shows and attendance

If you accept and don't come (and weren't on standby), you're marked as a **no-show** for that raid, and it counts against your attendance. Attendance over the last 30 days shows next to everyone's DKP, so decline if your plans change.

## If the raid starts without you

When an officer starts a raid you signed up for, and you're not in it, you get a reminder that it has started.

## Standby

If the raid is full, an officer can put you on **standby**. You earn the same DKP as the raid and it counts as attending.
