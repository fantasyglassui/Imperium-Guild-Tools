# Raids and Sign-ups

Open the **Raids** tab, pick a raid on the left, choose your role, and click **Accept**, **Tentative** or **Decline**.

## Signing up

1. Pick a raid on the left, then pick **Tank**, **Healer** or **DPS** on the switch under its details.
2. Click **Accept**, **Tentative** or **Decline**. You can change your answer any time before the raid.
3. If you accepted or are tentative, you get a reminder 15 minutes before the start, and an invite when officers send them.

Times are shown in your own time zone.

## Who's coming

Under the sign-up counts, the Raids tab lists who has accepted by class, and names in the sign-up list are in class colors. An orange warning shows what the raid is short of:

- **Tanks:** 2 wanted once 10 or more have accepted, otherwise 1.
- **Healers:** about one for every five players.
- **Classes:** once 10 have accepted, any class with max-level members in the guild that nobody has signed up with.

Click a name in the sign-up list to open their **player card** (their attendance, DKP and more).

Hover the warning for the full breakdown, including tentative sign-ups by role. Raids with gaps have an orange **!** in the Upcoming raids list. If you can play a role the raid is missing, now's the time to say so.

## No-shows and attendance

If you accept and don't come (and weren't on standby), you're marked as a **no-show** for that raid, and it counts against your attendance. Attendance over the last 30 days shows next to everyone's DKP, so decline if your plans change.

## If the raid starts without you

When an officer starts a raid you signed up for, and you're not in it, you get a reminder that it has started.

## Standby

If the raid is full, an officer can put you on **standby**. You earn the same DKP as the raid and it counts as attending.
