# Officer Raid Night Guide

Schedule the raid a day ahead, invite with one click, start the raid, auction the loot, and end the raid when you're done.

## Before raid night

1. **Schedule the raid.** On the **Raids** tab, click **New raid** and fill in the name, day, start time and any notes. Times are entered in your own time zone; everyone sees them in theirs.
2. **Check sign-ups** the day before. Pick the raid on the left to see who accepted, by role.
3. **Check versions.** On the **Addon** tab, filter to **Out of date** and remind those people to update.
4. **Set the DKP rules once** on the DKP tab (see [Officer DKP Management](Officer-DKP-Management)).

Only officers can schedule raids, change DKP or run auctions. The Guild Master chooses which ranks count as officers in **Settings**.

## Starting the raid

1. **Invite.** Select tonight's raid and click **Invite sign-ups**. It invites everyone online who accepted or is tentative, and turns the party into a raid automatically. Do it out of combat.
2. **Start the raid.** In **Raid night** at the bottom left, set the amounts, then click **Start raid**:

| Box | What it pays | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| On time | Once, to everyone in the raid when you click Start raid | 10 |
| Per hour | Every hour until you click End raid | 10 |
| Per boss | Each boss kill (not wipes), once per boss per night | 0 (off) |

3. **Add standby.** Click **Show current raid group**, type a name under **Standby** and click **Add to standby**. Standby members get the on-time DKP straight away and the hourly and boss DKP after that.

- **Stay in the raid.** Hourly and boss DKP come from the game of the officer who clicked Start raid. If you have to leave, end the raid and have another officer start it again.
- **Select the raid first.** Starting with the scheduled raid selected links sign-ups, reminders and no-shows to it.

## Running loot auctions

1. On the **DKP** tab, click in the **Item** box and Shift-click the item from the loot window or your bags. (Or type `/imperium auction`, Shift-click the item onto the end, add a minimum bid, and press Enter.)
2. Set the **Minimum bid** and the time (20 to 60 seconds), then click **Start auction**.
3. When time runs out, the winner is announced and charged. **Cancel auction** stops it with nobody charged.

If nobody bids, the raid gets a 20-second **free roll** instead. Open or hidden bids are set in the DKP rules.

## Fixing mistakes

| Mistake | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| Wrong amount or people on an award | DKP tab, **All changes**, right-click the change, **Undo**. Then award it correctly. |
| Item won by the wrong person, or traded afterwards | DKP tab, **Loot history**, right-click the item, type the new winner, **OK**. |
| Item should have been free | Same as above, but leave the name blank to refund it. |
| Someone missed DKP | Tick them (or **Select current raid**), enter the amount and a reason, **Add DKP**. |
| A bad PvP kill | Kills tab, right-click the kill, confirm. It's removed for the whole guild. |

Always give a reason when changing DKP by hand. It shows in everyone's history.

## Ending the night

Click **End raid** on the Raids tab. Until you do, hourly DKP keeps being paid. Then check the raid for **no-shows**: anyone who accepted but never came is marked on the sign-up list.
