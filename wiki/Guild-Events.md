# Guild Events

The **Events** tab is for everything that isn't a raid: PvP nights, world boss hunts, dungeon runs, guild meetings and get-togethers. Raids still go on the Raids tab, with sign-ups by role, DKP and raid nights.

## Planning an event
Anyone in the guild can plan one. Click **New event**, then:
1. Pick the kind of event: PvP, World boss, Dungeon, Meeting, Social or Other.
2. Give it a name, a day, a start time (24-hour, your time) and how long it lasts.
3. Add a note if you like (where to meet, what to bring), then **Create event**.

Guildmates online get an alert about it straight away. Guildmates who were offline see it when they log in.

## Answering
Pick an event on the left and click **Going**, **Maybe** or **Can't go**. You can change your answer any time; the latest one counts. Everyone going or maybe gets a reminder 15 minutes before it starts.

Don't want an alert every time someone plans an event? Untick **Tell me about new events** under the list. Reminders for events you're going to still come.

## On the day
Whoever planned the event, or any officer, can:
- **Invite going:** invites every online guildmate who answered Going or Maybe and isn't in your group yet.
- **Take attendance:** records everyone in your group (party or raid) as having come. Group up first.
- **Cancel event:** cancels it for everyone.

Events attended show on each member's player card, and count toward a recruit's trial.

## On the calendar
Events show on the Raids tab's **Calendar** in their own colours. Hover a day to see who's going; click an event to open it.

## Polls

Switch the Events tab to **Polls** (or `/warcrest polls`) to ask the guild something: which night for a new raid, where to hold the guild party.

- Click **New poll**, write the question and 2 to 6 answers, choose how long it stays open (1 day to a week), and click **Start poll**. The guild hears about it.
- **Vote** by clicking an answer. You can change your vote while it's open. Votes aren't secret: hover an answer to see who picked it.
- Whoever asked, or an officer, can **Close poll** early.
- Officers can always start polls. In **Settings**, they can tick **Members can start polls** (then Save for whole guild) to let everyone ask, one open poll each.
