# Officer DKP Management

The DKP tab's officer area holds the guild's DKP rules, backups, imports and seasons.

## Not using DKP?

If your guild hands out loot another way, untick **Use DKP for loot** in **Settings > Alerts** (Guild-wide) and click **Save for whole guild**, or pick **No DKP** in the welcome's guild setup (`/warcrest welcome`). The DKP tab, loot auctions and raid night DKP are then hidden for everyone. Raids, sign-ups, attendance, boss kills, wishlists and everything else still work. Tick it again any time to bring DKP back; nothing is lost.

## DKP rules (whole guild)

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Decay % and Every week | Takes that percentage off everyone's DKP after each Tuesday reset, applied by whichever officer is online first. **Decay now** applies it by hand. Each week can only be decayed once. |
| Hidden bids | Nobody sees anyone else's bid; the winner pays the second-highest bid + 1. |

## Awarding and correcting

- **Add DKP / Remove DKP:** tick members (or **Select current raid**), enter an amount and a reason.
- **Undo:** switch the view to **All changes** and right-click a change. Undone changes stay visible, marked as undone.
- **Loot fixes:** in **Loot history**, right-click an item to refund it or give it to someone else.

## Starting balances

Moving from another DKP system? Click **Import balances** and paste one member per line, such as `Alice, 120`. It previews what it found, flags lines it can't read, and sets each member's balance to that number.

## Backups

**Export DKP** gives a plain-text copy of standings, loot and every DKP change, ready to paste into a file or spreadsheet. Do this weekly.

## Alts

Members link their own alts (ask from the alt, confirm on the main). Officers can also link or unlink any alt from the **Alts** window. Alts share their main's DKP and attendance.

## Seasons

Every DKP change is kept, so after many months there's a lot to add up. **New season** keeps everyone's current DKP but archives the history behind it:

- **Kept:** everyone's DKP, alt links, DKP rules, and the last 30 days of raids and attendance.
- **Archived:** older DKP changes and loot history. They can't be undone or viewed in the addon afterwards.

**Export first.** A good rhythm is a new season with each raid tier, or every few months.

## Keeping it fair

Every raid, sign-up and DKP change goes into a shared record that is only ever added to. Only officers can change DKP, and an officer's change is only accepted straight from that officer or passed along by another officer, so nobody can fake DKP. Members catch up whenever an officer is online.
