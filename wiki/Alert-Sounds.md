# Alert Sounds

Every kind of alert has its own sound, so you can tell a guild kill from a wanted ganker without looking. Open **Settings > Sounds** to change any of them.

## Picking a sound

The Sounds view lists each kind of alert: guild kill, guild member down, wanted ganker spotted, bounty, kill streak, revenge, Top Head Hunters, boss down, raid, loot auction, achievement and guild news.

- Click the sound next to an alert to open the picker. Click any sound in the list to hear it and use it; type in the search box to find one.
- Click the play button to hear the current sound.
- **Use Warcrest's sounds again** puts every alert back to the sound it started with.

The picker has:

- **Warcrest's sounds**: 37 effects made for Warcrest, from war horns, fanfares and a heroic choir to war drums, sword clashes, bells, alarms, coins and a lute.
- **Your own sounds** (see below).
- **SharedMedia** sounds, if you use add-ons that share them.
- **The game's sounds**, such as the ready check, battleground ready and epic loot.
- **No sound**, to keep an alert quiet.

## Volume

**Channel** picks which of the game's volume sliders alert sounds follow. **Master** (the default) plays them even when game effects are turned down. Untick **Play alert sounds** to turn them all off.

## Your own sounds

1. In your game folder, go to `Interface\AddOns` and make a folder called `WarcrestSounds`.
2. Put your sound files in it. They need to be **.ogg** or **.mp3**.
3. **Restart the game.** The game only sees new files when it starts.
4. In Settings > Sounds, type the file name (like `horn.ogg`) under **Your own sounds** and click **Add**. Warcrest plays it to check it's there.
5. Your sound now appears in the picker under **Yours**.

The WarcrestSounds folder is yours: updating Warcrest never touches it. Removing a sound from the list doesn't delete the file.

If Warcrest says it couldn't play a file, check the file name (including .ogg or .mp3), that it's in `Interface\AddOns\WarcrestSounds`, and that you restarted the game after adding it.
