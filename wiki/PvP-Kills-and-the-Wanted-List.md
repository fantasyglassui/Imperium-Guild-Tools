# PvP Kills and the Wanted List

When a guild member kills an enemy player, the whole guild hears about it, and the best killers climb the monthly **Top Head Hunters** board.

## How a kill counts

- **You have to attack.** A kill counts only if you hit the enemy (a damaging spell, melee, shooting, or your pet) in the 10 seconds before they died. Being in the party or just targeting them isn't enough.
- **Killing blows and assists.** Whoever hit last gets the killing blow; everyone else who helped gets an assist.
- **No grey kills.** Kills of grey, much lower-level players are never announced or logged, so farming lowbies doesn't climb the board.

The game doesn't tell addons who landed the killing blow, so the addon works it out from what it can see. Turn on **enemy nameplates** (V) so it can see the fight.

## Top Head Hunters

The **Kills** tab lists every kill as a table (killer, victim, where and when) with assists or "Battleground" under the zone. On the right, a card shows who's **leading** and by how much, and the **Top Head Hunters** board ranks guild members by killing blows, with a bar for each place. Hover a name on the board to see their assists.

- Pick the period at the top: **This month**, **Last month**, **7 days** or **All time**. The dropdown beside it shows all kills, world PvP only, or battlegrounds only.
- The board **starts fresh on the 1st of each month** (realm time).
- On reset day, the guild hears last month's **top three**, and last month's champion is shown at the bottom of the board.

## The Wanted list

Enemy players who keep killing guild members in world PvP (3 times in 30 days by default) go on the **Wanted** tab.

- When anyone in the guild sees a wanted player, everyone gets an alert, and the spot is marked on the world map with a purple diamond for 30 minutes.
- **Kill a wanted player and the bounty is claimed:** they come off the list until they gank enough again.
- Right-click someone to hide them from your own list.

## Deaths on the map

When a guild member dies in world PvP, an X (or a skull) marks the spot on the world map, including inside OpenMap. Hover a mark for details; click it to set a TomTom waypoint if you use TomTom.
